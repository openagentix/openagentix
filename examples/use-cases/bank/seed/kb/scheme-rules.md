# Ficticard Scheme Dispute Rules

Ficticard is an invented card scheme. These rules describe no real scheme.

## Time limits

- A cardholder may dispute a charge up to 120 days after the charge. After that the dispute is out of time and the route is decline.
- The issuing bank must answer a new dispute within 10 working days of opening the case.
- A merchant bank has 30 days to answer a chargeback.

## Reason codes

| Code | Name | Typical claim |
|---|---|---|
| F10 | Not recognised | The cardholder did not make or allow the charge |
| P20 | Duplicate processing | The same charge was taken twice |
| S30 | Goods or services not received | Paid, nothing arrived |
| R40 | Cancelled subscription | Charged after a cancellation |
| A50 | Wrong amount | The amount is not what was agreed |

## Chargeback

A chargeback is a filing from the issuing bank to the merchant bank. It names the reason code, the amount, the transaction and the evidence. It is filed only after the disputes desk lead approves it.

## Declines

A decline is final for the scheme process. The letter to the cardholder gives the reason and says how to send new information.
