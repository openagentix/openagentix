# Fernvale Escalation Matrix

This matrix is invented for a demonstration.

| Situation | Who decides |
|---|---|
| Credit, chargeback filing or customer letter | Disputes desk lead (role desk-lead) |
| Missing evidence, unclear claim, or customer text holds an instruction | A disputes analyst reads the case |
| Five or more cases on one merchant or one device in 14 days | Fraud team lead |
| A case close to its response deadline with no decision | Disputes desk lead |

The agent never decides any of these. It prepares the case and asks.
