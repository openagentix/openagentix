# Fernvale Provisional Credit Rule

This rule is invented for a demonstration.

A provisional credit is a temporary credit to the cardholder's account while the chargeback runs.

## When it applies

- The route is duplicate charge, or a not-recognised charge with a pattern of fraud (for example a linked case on the same device).
- The charge is inside the scheme time limit.
- The account is active and the transaction has no earlier credit.
- The amount is not more than the disputed amount.

## When it does not apply

- Goods not received and wrong amount claims: the route is a chargeback with no credit.
- Out of time claims: the route is decline.
- A blocked or closed account gets no credit.

## Who posts it

The agent never posts a credit. The agent asks for approval. The disputes desk lead reads the exact request and approves it. The records service then posts the credit once. A second credit on the same transaction is refused.

## What the agent may say

Before approval, the agent may say that a credit is proposed and pending approval. It must never say that a credit was posted.
