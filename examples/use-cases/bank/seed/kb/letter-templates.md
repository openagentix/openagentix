# Customer Letter Templates and Tone

These templates are invented for a demonstration.

## Tone

- Plain, short sentences. No jargon. No blame.
- Say what we found, what happens next, and who decides.
- Never promise a result before the desk lead has approved it.
- Never say a credit was posted unless the letter is sent after approval and the credit exists.
- Do not repeat the customer's own words back as if they were facts.

## Provisional credit letter

"Dear cardholder, we reviewed your dispute about a charge of [amount]. [What we found.] If the disputes desk lead approves, we will credit [amount] to your account for now and ask the merchant bank to return the money. We will write again when the desk lead has decided."

## Decline letter

"Dear cardholder, we reviewed your dispute. [Reason, for example: the charge is more than 120 days old.] We cannot take this dispute forward. If you have new information, reply to this letter and an analyst will look at it."

## Inconclusive letter

No letter is drafted. An analyst writes to the customer after reading the case.
