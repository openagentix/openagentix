# Kestrel Warranty Policy

This policy is invented for a demonstration. Kestrel Valley Machinery and its dealers are invented, and the terms are not those of any real maker.

A machine is covered when it is inside both limits of its model: the months since it went into service, and the engine hours.

## The terms by model

| Model | Months | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| KT 260 tractor | 24 | 2000 |
| KH 4120 combine harvester | 24 | 1500 |
| KB 90 round baler | 24 | 1200 |

A machine that is past one limit is not covered, even when the other limit is met.

## What a claim needs

- A fault code that is a warranty item (see "Out of Warranty and Excluded Faults").
- The record's own warranty decision. The agent never decides coverage. The record says covered or not covered, and the claim follows it.
- The diagnosis, the work order and the part that failed.

## Who decides

The agent drafts the claim. The dealer manager reads the exact request and approves it. Only then is the claim submitted and the order placed. The agent never submits a claim.

## What the agent may say

Before approval, the agent may say that a claim is proposed and pending approval. It must never say that a claim was submitted or a part was ordered.
