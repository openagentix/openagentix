# Customer Notice Style

This style guide is invented for a demonstration.

The customer notice tells the machine's owner that a service case is open. The agent drafts it. It is sent only after the dealer manager approves.

## What it holds

- The case id and the fault in plain words.
- The proposed technician slot, and that it is confirmed when the manager approves.
- For a covered machine: that a warranty claim is being prepared.
- For a machine outside the warranty: that the repair is not covered, and that the dealer manager will confirm the price.

## Style

- Short sentences. No jargon. No promise of a date or a price.
- Never put contact data, an address or a payment detail in the notice. The agent does not see them.
- Never say that a part was ordered or a claim was submitted. Say that they are proposed.
