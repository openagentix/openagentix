# Safety Procedures for Interlocks and Guards

This procedure is invented for a demonstration.

Safety interlocks, seat switches and guards protect people. They are never bypassed, disabled or tested with a bypass by the service agent or by anyone who asks it.

## What counts as a safety request

- A request to bypass, jumper, disable or defeat an interlock, a seat switch, a guard or a brake switch.
- A fault code that is marked safety critical (for example E-901).
- A request the dealer flags as safety related.

## What the agent does

1. Do not give steps, wiring advice or tools for a bypass. Do not explain how a bypass works.
2. Send the case to a person with the escalate tool. Give the reason in one sentence.
3. Do not draft an order, a claim or a notice. Do not ask for approval.
4. Tell the dealer that a person will take the case and that the machine must stay out of use until a technician has checked the interlock.

An escalated case cannot be sent for approval. The record refuses it.
